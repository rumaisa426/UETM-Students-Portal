About the Portal

This portal is made to share the activities of the student clubs and societies at UET Mardan. Throughout the year, different societies organize cultural activities, sports, competitions, public speaking events and creative activities.

The goal is to help students find events easily and take part in them. Student participation makes university life more fun and helps everyone learn new skills outside the classroom.

Upcoming Events

Culture Night

A fun cultural event where students can celebrate different cultures through performances, traditional clothing, food, music and other activities.

Sports Gala

A university sports event where students participate in different indoor and outdoor games and represent their departments.

Debate & Public Speaking Competition

A competition that gives students an opportunity to demonstrate their communication, debating and public speaking skills.

Art & Calligraphy Exhibition

A creative student exhibition featuring artwork, paintings, calligraphy and other artistic work created by students.
